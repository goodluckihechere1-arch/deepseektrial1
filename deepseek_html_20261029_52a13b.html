Residential plumbing

For apartments, townhouses and brownstones

From a single dripping faucet to a full bathroom gut renovation, we handle residential plumbing with the same care we would want in our own homes. Booties on, badge visible, uniform clean — every visit.

  • Water heaters — repair, replacement and correct sizing for tank and tankless systems
  • Plumbing remodeling — kitchens and baths with licensed plumbing from rough-in to finish
  • New plumbing fixtures — toilets, tubs, showers and shower valves
  • Faucets, sinks and garbage disposals — supplied and installed correctly
  • Leak detection — finding hidden leaks with minimal disruption
  • Water quality — filtration and treatment for taste, odor and hardness

Residential heating

Heat that works before you need it most

NYC heating systems take a beating — old piping, hard water, seasonal startups and radiators that have not been balanced in decades. We repair, replace and tune residential heating systems so you are not making the call in January.

  • Boiler repair and replacement for homes and small multi-family buildings
  • Radiators and baseboard — balancing, valve repair and replacement
  • Seasonal tune-ups to catch problems before the first cold snap
  • Local Law 152 gas inspections for qualifying buildings
  • Preventative maintenance plans with scheduled inspections

Commercial plumbing

Buildings, restaurants and retail

Commercial plumbing is judged on downtime. We work with owners, managers and building supers to schedule work around business hours, keep documentation clean, and close out violations so they do not come back.

  • Commercial plumbing service and repair for restaurants, retail, offices and multi-family
  • Backflow prevention and testing with documentation for your records and your filings
  • Code violations — resolving DOB and DEP plumbing violations and closing them out
  • Local Law 152 gas piping inspections for covered buildings
  • Preventative maintenance plans built around your building's schedule
